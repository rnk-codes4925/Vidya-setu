Light – Reflection and Refraction

CBSE Class 10 Science • Chapter 9 / 10

1. Laws of Reflection

Light is a form of energy that enables us to see objects. The two fundamental laws of reflection are:

  • First Law: The angle of incidence ($i$) is equal to the angle of reflection ($r$).
  • Second Law: The incident ray, the reflected ray, and the normal to the reflecting surface at the point of incidence, all lie in the same plane.
∠ i = ∠ r

2. Spherical Mirrors & Mirror Formula

For concave and convex spherical mirrors, the focal length ($f$), image distance ($v$), and object distance ($u$) satisfy the mirror formula:

1/f = 1/v + 1/u

Magnification ($m$): Ratio of height of image ($h'$) to height of object ($h$):

m = h' / h = -v / u

3. Refraction & Snell's Law

The bending of light when it passes from one transparent medium to another of different optical density.
